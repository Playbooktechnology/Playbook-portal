# The editorial gate — does this story earn space at all

(Moat Playbook guide, 2026-09-13.) Before routing a format, decide whether the
story earns a Playbook article in the first place. This runs at **Step 0.5**,
after the overlap check (`overlap-check.md`, which asks "does this already run
on the site") and before the format router (`format-tiers.md` §1, which asks
"how much depth"). This file answers a third, prior question: **"does this
deserve Playbook's byline at all, and what can we add?"**

Playbook no añade profundidad por obligación. Añade valor. Toda pieza necesita
delta Playbook. No toda pieza necesita profundidad Playbook.

## What does NOT earn publication by itself

A comunicado existing, another outlet having covered it, the story trending,
a big number appearing in it, a new sponsorship, having several links, being
able to build a chart, or being able to invent a México/LATAM connection.
None of these are, on their own, a reason to publish.

## What does

At least: (1) suficiente importancia editorial, and (2) algo útil que
Playbook pueda aportar. **No exige scoop. Sí exige exclusividad de valor** —
information propia, un dato relevante, contexto, un antecedente, una
consecuencia, un mecanismo, economics, incentivos, control, riesgo,
propiedad, distribución, poder de negociación, benchmark, comparable, una
pregunta nueva, o identificar con claridad qué información importante
todavía falta. Never add one of these elements only to demonstrate research
— see `voice-and-style.md` §11, "Qué se queda fuera".

## The output

Produce this block before moving to the router. It is prose judgment, not a
form to fill mechanically — a weak answer here is itself the signal to stop.

```
DECISIÓN: PUBLICAR / NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA / RADAR
RAZÓN:
READER PERSONA PRIMARIO:
NECESIDAD DEL READER: ¿Qué necesita entender, decidir, comparar, anticipar o aprender?
DELTA PLAYBOOK: ¿Cuál es concretamente el valor que podemos añadir?
EVIDENCIA DISPONIBLE:
DATOS / EVIDENCIA QUE FALTAN:
```

If `DECISIÓN: PUBLICAR`, continue to the router (`format-tiers.md` §1), which
asks its own, parallel question block (reader persona carries over — see
below).

## How this differs from the other two gates

- **`overlap-check.md`** asks whether this exact story already runs on the
  site. It still runs at Step 0, unchanged, regardless of what this gate
  decides — a story can pass this gate and still be killed by overlap.
- **`postura-editorial.md`**'s relevancia-baja/sensibilidad-alta cell already
  says "probablemente NO publicar — porque la historia no alcanza el
  estándar editorial, no por la relación." This gate is that same judgment,
  made explicit and run on **every** story, not only ones that touch a
  relationship. The two overlap on purpose; run both where a story qualifies
  for postura-editorial.md's sensitivity review too.

## For a zero-review funnel (`publish-newsletter`)

This funnel has no downstream human approval — Step 8 is the only place a
story can be declined, and it is this one. `NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA` or `RADAR`
here means: do not draft, do not proceed to the router, and say so plainly in
the run report (which item, and why) instead of shipping an article for it.

## For a human-reviewed funnel (`publish-sourced-article`)

Same gate, same output block, produced before Step 3. A `RADAR` /
`NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA` decision here means Step 8's human review simply never
happens for that link — there is no draft to review. Say so in the run's
summary rather than silently dropping the link.

## Reader personas

Every article carries an internal primary reader persona — it does not have
to appear on the site. Pick one from:

- Decision Maker
- Operator / Builder
- Commercial Leader
- Investor / Advisor
- Emerging Leader
- Student / Future Industry Professional
- Sports Lover / Backstage Curious
- Opportunity Seeker / Entrepreneur
- Amplifier / Interpreter

**Do not write for all of them at once.** The persona decides: qué pregunta
responder, qué puede darse por sentado, qué explicar, qué datos son
relevantes, qué dejar fuera, qué consecuencia importa. **Rigor doesn't change
by persona. The level and type of explanation does.** Carry the same persona
from this gate into the router's own `READER PERSONA PRIMARIO` field and into
the Moat Check's question 2 (`moat-check.md`) — it's one decision, referenced
three times, not re-derived per step.
