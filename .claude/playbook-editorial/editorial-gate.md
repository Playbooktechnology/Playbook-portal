# The editorial gate — does this story earn space in Playbook?

(Moat Playbook, 2026-09-13.) A decision that runs **before** the router
(`format-tiers.md` §1), not instead of it. The router assumes the answer to
"should this become an article" is already yes and asks how deep; this gate
is what produces that yes. It is also not the sensitivity check
(`postura-editorial.md`), which is a different axis entirely — sensitivity
asks *how carefully*, this gate asks *whether at all*. A story can clear this
gate cleanly and still need protocolo amarillo, and a story that fails this
gate never reaches that question.

**The principle.** Playbook doesn't add depth out of obligation. It adds
value. A story can resolve in 120 words or need 1,200+, but after passing
through Playbook the reader has to know, understand, connect or question
something the headline, press release or original source didn't already give
them on its own.

> Toda pieza necesita delta Playbook. No toda pieza necesita profundidad
> Playbook.

---

## 1. What does NOT earn a story its place

None of the following, alone, is a reason to publish (all real failure
modes, the same family `format-tiers.md` §1 already bans from *deciding the
format* — this is the same discipline one step earlier, applied to *whether
to write at all*):

- a press release exists;
- another outlet already covered it;
- it's trending;
- there's a big number in it;
- a new sponsorship was announced;
- there are several links about it;
- a chart could be made from it;
- a connection to México/LATAM can be invented for it.

A scoop is not required. **Exclusivity of value is required** — the reader
has to leave with something the source alone didn't give them.

---

## 2. The two conditions to advance

To clear the gate, both have to hold:

1. **Sufficient editorial importance** — it moves money, power, governance,
   rights, competition, structure, or a decision the sports-business industry
   needs to track. (The same importance test `postura-editorial.md` §1 uses
   for relevance, asked here independent of any relationship to Playbook.)
2. **Something Playbook can genuinely add** — one of: información propia, un
   dato relevante, contexto, un antecedente, una consecuencia, un mecanismo,
   economics, incentivos, control, riesgo, propiedad, distribución, poder de
   negociación, benchmark, comparable, conexión, cálculo, contradicción,
   implicación, una pregunta nueva, o identificar con claridad qué
   información importante todavía falta.

**Never add one of these elements just to demonstrate research.** If the
honest answer to condition 2 is "we'd only be rewriting the release," that is
the gate telling you this item doesn't clear it, not a prompt to manufacture
an angle.

---

## 3. The decision

Three outcomes, same posture as `overlap-check.md`'s four outcomes: most of
them mean the item does not become an article today, and that is work done,
not work missing.

- **PUBLICAR** — both conditions hold. Continue to the router
  (`format-tiers.md` §1).
- **RADAR / SEGUIMIENTO** — importance is real but the second condition isn't
  there yet (evidence too thin, the story too early, Playbook has nothing to
  add today that a wire brief doesn't already say). Worth tracking, not worth
  writing today. Say so in the run report rather than silently dropping it —
  a running story that later clears the gate should read as a follow-up
  (`voice-and-style.md` §10), which requires someone to have noticed it the
  first time.
- **NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA** — either condition genuinely fails: importance is
  too thin for the audience, or nothing distinguishes what Playbook would
  publish from the source. Not a permanent verdict on the story, a verdict on
  today's version of it.

---

## 4. Required output

Every item run through this gate produces exactly this block, whether or not
it advances:

```
DECISIÓN: PUBLICAR / NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA / RADAR
RAZÓN:
READER PERSONA PRIMARIO:
NECESIDAD DEL READER: ¿Qué necesita entender, decidir, comparar, anticipar o aprender?
DELTA PLAYBOOK: ¿Cuál es concretamente el valor que podemos añadir?
EVIDENCIA DISPONIBLE:
DATOS / EVIDENCIA QUE FALTAN:
```

**`READER PERSONA PRIMARIO`** — pick one from `voice-and-style.md` §13.
Naming it here, before the router, is what keeps the router's own
`READER PERSONA PRIMARIO` field (`format-tiers.md` §1) an inherited decision
rather than a second, possibly different guess at the same question three
steps later.

---

## How a skill run uses this

Run this **after** research (so there's evidence to judge) and **before**
the router (so format is never decided on a story that shouldn't run at
all). In `publish-newsletter` — the funnel with no human review — a
RADAR/SEGUIMIENTO or NO PUBLICAR TODAVÍA verdict means that item does not
continue to the router or drafting steps; it is skipped exactly the way an
overlap-check outcome A item is skipped, and reported the same way
(`publishing-mechanics.md`, "Report back"). No one has to approve the skip
in real time — the report is the record. In `publish-sourced-article` the
same verdict applies before drafting starts, ahead of the human review gate
at Step 8; it saves a full draft round when the story was never going to
clear review on value grounds, though the human retains the final call at
Step 8 as always.
